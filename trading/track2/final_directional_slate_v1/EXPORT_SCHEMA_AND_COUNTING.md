# Export keys, units and supported counting

Canonical numeric analysis is analysis_03/. Source run CSV summaries contain
risk-arm labels EQUAL/ROLLING_RISK/EWMA_RISK/S2C_RISK and population MATCHED/
ATTAINABLE. Raw cohort exports also preserve old book fields arm (full account
ID) and population (economic mode). These overwrote similarly named wrapper
labels. The analysis maps both through the unambiguous sealed case key and keeps
book_arm/economic_mode separately. Earlier failed empty analysis_01/02 and logs
remain; no raw result was overwritten and no financial run was repeated.

One cohort is an immutable virtual entry; actual physical orders and fill portions
are different units. Internal offsets pay no external fee. Any-risk closures,
final-risk role and a policy final exit after an earlier partial forced exit
can differ. Dynamic early_exits legacy field compares against origin and can be0
with many risk closures; use true ages and explicit risk counts, not a blanket
'premature' claim. Fixed T480 has a real scheduled endpoint and risk preemption.

For F3, the exact count with any risk portion is supported. In308 captured fixed
cohorts a prior forced portion was followed by final policy closure. Earlier
name/vintage trigger detail was compacted; generic RISK_OTHER_CAP for these rows
means prior-risk constraint NOT_IDENTIFIABLE, not demonstrated insolvency or an
aggregate-cap cause. FIXED_POLICY_RISK_CAUSE_SUPPORT.csv reports these counts per
population/arm. Do not sum overlapping name/vintage counts as exclusive exits.
The old V2 parent has supported grouped early-name959, early-vintage307 and
scheduled/late-name7 counts, not unique marked-breach decision counts. Old per-lot
net tails are unavailable. These scoped attribution limits do not change the
reconciled book, selected policy, orders, risk protection, gross/actions or net.

Dynamic native cause labels retain prior risk when a later portion has a different
policy reason, with whole-account causal attribution caveats. New matched-source
opportunities are common but filled paths differ through quantities, rounding,
capital, duplicate suppression and marked-risk exits. Daily return contrasts are
descriptive dependent account-day pairs, not independent statistical observations.

Support below80 funded matched cohorts is explicitly low for R07 (60); the80
heuristic applied to selecting its94-cohort original parent, not to inventing more
sizing trades. Zero/undefined metrics are N/A, never censored prices replaced by0.
