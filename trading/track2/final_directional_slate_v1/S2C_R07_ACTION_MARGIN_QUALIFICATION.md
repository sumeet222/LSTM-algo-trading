# R07 incremental margin versus rolling: important qualification

On MATCHED opportunities, S2-C beats rolling net by$127.68. Its raw signed-price
gross is actually$21.80 LOWER. Signed action value is$153.98 HIGHER, and total
costs about$4.50 HIGHER, yielding the$127.68 difference. These are exact arithmetic
components of the separately funded books, not a frictionless/counterfactual rerun.

Thus the narrow advantage over rolling is action/holding/position-path dependent,
not a demonstrated superior raw directional edge. Ordinary action evidence includes
single-provider and conditional cases under inherited research policy. The result
is fragile to independent action/entitlement verification and tiny relative to
sampling, survivor/selection and quote/locate uncertainty. No action assumption
was changed to obtain it. Independent recomputation belongs in Stage6.

See S2C_INCREMENTAL_PNL_COMPONENTS.csv. This strengthens the main report's mixed,
sparse and concentrated interpretation; it does not alter selection or any result.
