# Directional research handoff

**All 18 strategies and all three V3 regimes received the offline screen.
No final baseline is selected. S2-C sizing remains on HOLD.** This
analysis measures later outcomes of recorded exits from a repeatedly
researched 2021–2024 survivor panel. It does not simulate a feasible
continuation portfolio.

## What is established

All 54 original account money and exit-count identities passed.
There were 158,118 signed virtual exit portions, producing 474,354
predeclared targets. They shared 231,010 raw-reference keys, including
original entry-marker support. Valid reference count was 474,109.
There were 70 cutoff targets and 175 unsupported MRK/OGN distribution
crossings. The five-minute, within-target-session search had no
remaining missing or over-limit marks in this source. Supported
ordinary dividends and splits were applied, with signed cash and
claims separate. Reference times and availability appear in the
hashed sample.

The exact previously funded positives are V2 M04/T480 +USD 1,454.023964,
R07/D3 +USD 1,845.214888, R07/D5 +USD 2,574.485154, V3 R01/H85
+USD 10,488.392222, and R07/H85 +USD 117.009705. All other configurations
retain their losses. V1 had no positive net strategy.

The original executed-exit-anchor pooled means independently reproduced
to numerical precision: reversed +5.281642, weak +4.298624, strong
+2.338254 and unknown +3.349880 bps at +240. Those labels are descriptive
saved scores, sometimes stale at submission. New same-share raw-to-raw
means, with changed action support and search policy, were approximately
+1.84, +0.26, −1.46 and −0.14 bps. This removes original exit-concession
recovery and includes newly supported action crossings. The difference
prevents reading the old pooled means as tradable signal-state forecasts.

## Mechanism families for a later fixed design

Keep all 18 as context. The following five families organize a small
proposed research slate. They are not five selected profit claims.

1. **Confirmed gap continuation, M04.** The prior 480-minute net gain
   was positive in two years and concentrated in AMD/TSLA. Dynamic V3
   H85 still lost USD 2,118. Nonforced external +240 conditional
   continuation was USD 3,325, and a small event-resolution component
   was positive. A fixed, two-valid-check confirmation of failure
   back to the frozen opening reference is the most direct next
   exit hypothesis. Competing evidence is the large realized loss
   among failed gaps, which waiting can worsen.
2. **Opening-range and intraday continuation, M05/M01 with M06 as
   compact context.** Repeated weakening or resolution can create
   small expensive tickets. M05 nonforced +240 and next-session raw
   margins survived top-five/top-two omission. The actual portfolio
   still lost USD 21,100. Test persistence only while the original
   breakout or aligned sign remains intact, retaining all hard
   exits. M06's strongest apparent continuation partly follows
   infeasible risk reductions and is not a cap bypass.
3. **Opening-gap fade, M03.** The successful-resolution group was
   profitable while failed-fade tails lost much more. The
   post-resolution +240 effect was small. A fixed gap-volatility
   bound on entry-time trailing distance is a risk-tail hypothesis,
   with recovering fades as the opposing mechanism. Delaying every
   completed gap fill is unsupported.
4. **Compression breakout, R07.** Fixed 3/5-session results were
   small, sparse and TSLA-concentrated, with 60–67% early closure.
   H85 dynamic profit vanished after one large raw winner was
   removed arithmetically. Nonforced next-session continuation was
   favorable but +60/+240 was negative. A two-daily-check channel
   failure confirmation remains a low-priority, separately fixed
   hypothesis requiring broader support.
5. **Intermediate momentum and portfolio headroom, R01.** Preserve
   the completed deep dive and one-v20 vintage-reserve proposal.
   The H85 gain was long- and two-name-concentrated. Shared
   opportunities implicate exit/risk feedback, with contradictory
   ordinary-versus-risk subsets. Conditional continuation does
   not establish a better directional model.

## One next experiment

If separately approved, begin with **M04 unchanged V3 H85 versus M04 H85
requiring two consecutive valid monitor failures of the original
entry-day opening reference for EVENT_RESOLVED**. Reset that confirmation
on missing refresh. Keep reversal, weakening, trail, 780-minute maximum
and mandatory risk unchanged, even when they preempt the second check.
Keep entries, six-ticket budget, micro costs, conditional shorts, receipt,
raw-share actions, 10% name/20% vintage/100% gross limits and continuous
USD 100,000 accounts fixed. Do not combine this with a buffer or cost
change in the same primary comparison.

Fix an untouched/prospective period with at least 52 eligible full
weekly sessions represented, then examine all eligible M04 origins.
Log source-key paired eligibility, gross and net, executed turnover,
time invested, every exit reason, drawdown, conditional action value
and exact cohort allocations. Net per marked gross RTH dollar-year and
per external turnover must improve. Falsify the mechanism if an improved
win rate or lower ticket count fails to cover additional downside,
carry and price costs. An advantage confined to one chronological half
or the top two names remains fragile. The original V2/V3 protocols
supply immutable context. No threshold may be selected from this audit.

## Limits and audit status

The full deep dives were M04 and R07, plus M01/M03/M05 under the
predeclared depth cap. R01 received a delta only. M06/R09 and remaining
IDs have compact review. No observed special action was silently priced
as zero. Exact per-cohort net allocations were compacted. First-entry
raw reconstruction matched 31 accounts; 23 micro cases had small
USD 0.315–35.73 residuals, so their individual raw tails are explicitly
unsupported. Exact frozen group and account sums remain usable.
This is an attribution-field limitation, with a bounded future journal
extraction path, not evidence of a newly unbalanced funded book.

Risk-forced opportunity is separately infeasible. M01/M03 risk
continuation was often adverse, and R03's favorable risk panel
contradicted its nonforced panel. Fewer forced exits are therefore not
automatically better. Short borrow, fee floors, internal netting,
whole-account liquidation and reused capital change a feasible path.
The standalone future-exit sensitivity models none of that joint
capital feedback. OHLCV is a trade-print proxy with assumed price
concession and impact, no observed NBBO or locates. Stage 6,
independent confirmation, S2-C sizing and funded refinement were not run.
