# Prespecified screening rules

This is an offline analysis of existing funded outputs. No funded replay,
parameter search, new neural inference or baseline selection is authorized.

## References and units

The population is every sealed V3 virtual exit leg, including partials,
for the exact 18 IDs and 3 regimes. A stable ID binds case, lot, effective
minute and the deterministic source row index. Retain actual exit signed
quantity, raw and modeled fill, decision, observation/posting and causal
strength when known. A leg's risk cause and its parent's eventual cause
can differ. Raw dollars from separate accounts or horizons are never
treated as portfolio cash.

Use exactly 60 and 240 scheduled XNYS minutes, plus the same opening-offset
minute in the next full 390-minute XNYS session. The latter preserves V3's
declared next-full-session diagnostic, including its early-close skip.
An exact scheduled close maps to the next RTH open. For each target,
take the first source-valid observed raw minute open at or after target
within five scheduled minutes and within that target session. Publish
its actual effective minute, completion at +1, availability at +3 and
target delay. No 2025 market data. Missing/late observations are censored.

Apply verified raw-share split ratios and signed ordinary ex-date
entitlements in chronological order between exit and reference. Preserve
paid cash versus unpaid claims at the reference. Missing terms for special
distributed assets cause censoring. MRK/OGN crossing is censored here.
Retain original event confidence classes and approved conditional ordinary
no-event assumptions. No invented dividend or special value is permitted.

Use both raw-to-raw signed price movement and the saved executed-exit
anchor. Report original exit-concession recovery, a separately assumed
later exit concession and a frozen-exit-mark 2% ACT/365 standalone short
carry proxy. Fee differences for a standalone leg are a separate sensitivity,
with no claim to actual account fee allocation or netting. These conditional
numbers are neither funded portfolio outcomes nor exact counterfactual costs.

## Evidence hierarchy

A. ACTUAL_POSITIVE_EXPLORATORY attaches to exact reconciled positive
executed configurations only. Include every positive V2/V3 configuration.
R07 and M04 always receive full descriptive dives, with R01 delta-only.

B. DIAGNOSTICALLY_INTERESTING requires a named causal mechanism, adequate
nonforced support, positive raw-to-raw and separately cost-aware opportunity
in at least two years and two of the three frozen horizons, and explicit
side/name/year concentration checks. Use 100 valid nonforced legs across
the period as a support flag, never a significance threshold. Findings
confined to risk-forced or internal exits remain hypotheses with low
economic feasibility. Before promotion, inspect top 1/5/10 observations
and top two tickers, which may remove the sign of the diagnostic. Retain
both missed upside and avoided losses.

C. NEGATIVE_OR_INCONCLUSIVE retains every other strategy in the final
discussion. A low-quality or absent effect is not an exclusion from research.

All 18 receive the same small, fixed descriptive screen. No horizon,
threshold, sample or candidate rule is tuned after reading outcomes.
At most three additional strategies receive full dives. Priority among
qualifying candidates is a concrete source-supported exit/cost mechanism,
then broad year/side support and modest concentration, with ID order as
a final tie-break. Others receive compact notes. High evidence quality
means reconciled economics and directly traceable fields. Medium means
reconciled aggregates with missing per-cohort detail. Low means sparse,
concentrated, infeasible or unsupported inference. Two-year support remains
a fragility marker in the reused 2021–2024 survivor panel.

No oracle Sharpe, combined portfolio NAV, independent p-value or feasible
profit claim is computed. The later baseline slate and all refined rules
remain proposals requiring separate authorization and untouched evidence.
