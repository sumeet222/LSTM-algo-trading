# Future sizing interface

The pure sizing adapter is lstm_data.trading.track2.stage35.arms.size_multiplier.
For a separately authorized comparison, supply the decision, the cached
original F21–F24 forecast, the frozen fold/ticker/slot prefix RiskReference and
the matured control history. Join by frozen ticker, nominal UTC origin, fold
and readiness timestamp after freezing the new directional rule. The adapter
supports EQUAL, S2C_RISK, ROLLING_RISK and EWMA_RISK and refuses incomplete or
immature inputs. The V2 signal/control cache and its model and risk-reference
lineage remain the authority for the follow-on.
