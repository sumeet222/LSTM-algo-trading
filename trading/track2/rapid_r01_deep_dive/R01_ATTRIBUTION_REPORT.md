## Most of the net gain came from the long side and two names

Conservative longs earned **USD 15,702.57 net** on 248 cohorts. Shorts lost
**USD 5,214.18 net** on 250. Initial raw notional totaled USD 1.52M long and
USD 1.55M short. The long side supplied USD 19,036 of USD 19,099 gross. Shorts
generated USD 63 gross, paid USD 861 net action value and USD 607 borrow, and
absorbed ordinary execution costs. A contemporaneous daily regression
against the equal-weight split-consistent survivor price proxy gave a beta
of +0.0052 over 1,004 paired days. Standard and high-risk had −0.0045 and
+0.0051. These are whole-account descriptive betas against the narrow
local panel. There is no certified sector map or intraday factor-exposure
archive. Pre-entry peer-state buckets use previous completed-session
returns and exclude the candidate name.

TSLA contributed USD 6,777 net across 66 cohorts, and AVGO USD 4,612 across
38. The remaining names sum to **−USD 901 net**. COP added USD 2,386.
MRK lost USD 1,111, including −USD 418 of actions. The special action did
not create a windfall. Arithmetic ticker omissions retain the frozen
positions and do not model alternative orders, capital or exits.

The 229 gross winners averaged USD 334.48 and the 268 gross losers averaged
−USD 214.54. One lot was flat. Hit rate was 45.98%, payoff ratio 1.56,
profit factor 1.33 and median −USD 25.06. After ordinary execution fees and
price concessions, before actions and carry, the hit rate was 45.18% and
the profit factor 1.19. Fully net cohort tails are unavailable because
complete per-lot action/carry allocations were compacted.

The first and second halves gained USD 6,763 and USD 3,726 respectively.
2022 and 2024 combined supplied USD 10,648, about 102% of full-period net.
The peak before maximum drawdown was 2022-11-03. The trough was 2023-03-31
and the peak recovered on 2024-03-04. The longest underwater interval was
331 sessions. Standard and high-risk remained below their January 2021
peak for 989 sessions. Month and quarter dollar changes use daily marked
NAV. Cohort entry and exit month tables assign realized price outcomes to
the respective stamps and should not be added to NAV time attribution.

In conservative, the first-ranked same-sign candidate earned USD 4,655
gross per USD 1M raw turnover versus USD 1,855 for second-ranked. The lowest
standardized-strength decile earned USD 8,461 per USD 1M, and the highest
earned USD 4,477. Intermediate deciles vary. The same 832 causal candidate
scores define deciles across regimes for description. The strength
relationship does not support adding a higher score threshold. Last
supported monitor ranks 1–2 accounted for USD 12,000 of gross across 327
cohorts, while ranks 5–20 contributed USD 6,335 across 56. The rank
timestamp is the last observable eligible checkpoint up to the exit
submission. A previously latched trigger can be older.

H85 cohorts held >1,950 scheduled minutes contributed USD 53,276 gross;
shorter cohorts lost USD 34,177. These are realized-duration strata shaped
by stops, caps and price paths. Holding winners longer by construction
would require seeing those paths in advance. Overnight exposure comprised
478 of 498 H85 cohorts, including weekends and shortened sessions.
