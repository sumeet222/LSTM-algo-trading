# Raw-share source certification and limits

`RAW_SHARE_SOURCE_COVERAGE_V3.json` verifies unchanged SHA256/mtime for48 OOS
canonical-minute partitions. This is identity/lineage certification, not a scan
certifying every economic bar. The sealed bounded plan projected11 exact minute
keys across5 partitions;all11 passed raw OHLCV/time validation,none was missing.
No partition was rewritten or expanded with new prices. No2025+ prices were read.

The original canonical minute source precedes `hf1m_action_source.adjust_prices`.
The modeling hourly store is retrospective split-consistent and cannot be used
for physical cash/share accounting. The original source/adjustment code and facts
remain hash-bound. At ingestion require positive finite OHLC,low<=open/close<=high,
nonnegative volume,unique timestamp,regular-session validity,original observed
vintage and authorized partition. Source holes/invalid/corrected prices remain
explicit,not profitable-date filters. Existing spike/quarantine classifications
are inherited unchanged;genuine large market moves are not erased.

The bounded examples cover AAPL2020Aug28/Aug31 raw4:1 units;TSLA2022Aug24/Aug25
raw3:1 units;AAPL2024May9/10/16 cash-dividend context;BA common-stock nonpayer;
COP ordinary source;MRK2021Jun2/3 special-action context. Ratios test units,not an
assertion that overnight market movement is exactly a split factor. Apple issuer
cash records use $.82 before and$.205 after its2020 split,consistent raw-share
units. AVGO's provider cash is5.25 before its2024 split and.53 afterward;the
10:1 original fact is retained,not a claim that those payments are identical.

A minute[start,start+1m) is consumed after completion and modeled receipt. The
new raw-observation fixture conservatively uses completion+2 wall minutes;
1/2/5 variants are tested. This is separate from original signal nominal+2
minimum issuance,which also waits for every genuine ingredient's readiness.
Neither is a measured vendor latency. A pre-submitted order may be modeled at a
later minute open,but that open is posted only after completion/receipt. Prior
received volume controls the1% capacity proxy,never fill-bar future volume.

Corporate action units and source truth remain separate from adjusted model
features. Ex-date entitlement is raw shares immediately before ex;settlement
moves the claim,not the share basis. An old raw mark unit-converted at a split
keeps its old timestamp and a factor/source receipt;it remains stale until a real
new raw observation. Fractional CIL cannot be manufactured from that old mark.

Strict source/publication PIT truth,actual quotes/locates and complete special
asset valuation are not certified. MRK's OGN claim has no approved OGN raw tape,
CIL price or complete basis/due-bill certification. Its affected inventory/window
is UNRESOLVED_SPECIAL_ACTION and blocks authoritative financial release. COP2012
PSX remains an inherited prefix qualification,not a new OOS ordinary event.
