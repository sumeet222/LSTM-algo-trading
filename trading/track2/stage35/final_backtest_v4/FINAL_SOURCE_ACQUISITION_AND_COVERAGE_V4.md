# Source acquisition and economic coverage

The initial read-only preflight processed 1,005 sessions, 7,014 legal nominal origins and 140,280 stock opportunities across the original 20 names and four annual folds. It verified 131,111 cached original C-A/O1/FT16/CLOCK/C3 forecasts, 128,192 EQUAL/S2C candidates and 87,557 matched four-arm/control candidates. This is cached-signal verification, not a repeated neural inference campaign. Existing original archive forecast parity remains HISTORICAL_PREDICTION_PARITY_NOT_COMPARABLE.

Forty-eight original 2021–2024 canonical-minute partitions contained 7,813,800 scheduled ticker-minute cells: 7,714,752 valid and 99,048 invalid/missing under the fixed OHLCV/time checks. The original source partitions were not changed. The inherited 18 raw quarantine keys all precede 2021; their dispositions remain unchanged. Source execution prices precede adjust_prices; adjusted model-hourly values are never used as share/cash prices.

ECONOMIC_GAP_SOURCE_PLAN_V4.json was frozen before supplementary prices. Every exact invalid/missing key was selected across all 20 names and all sessions, independently of positions, returns, endpoint completeness or F09. Each dated key projection was sealed before its request. The raw SIP endpoint was queried for those keys using adjustment=raw, one-minute intervals and the existing configured entitlement. Only exact selected rows were retained; valid original bars were never replaced. Full upstream response digests, retrieval/query/status receipts and projected-file hashes are preserved. This avoids duplicating a replacement full panel. Provider data are retrospective collection, not measured historical receipt.

The completed inventory covers 1,004 dates with at least one gap: 1,004 successful HTTP responses supplied 88,674 usable missing raw bars; 10,374 keys still lacked a usable returned bar. The remaining gaps are not automatically no-trade, halt or cancellation certificates. They never suppress earlier forecast opportunities. ECONOMIC_GAP_COVERAGE_BY_STOCK_YEAR_V4.json reports each stock/year. Supplemented economic observations become available only after their own bar completion plus the scenario lag; their volume cannot authorize their own fill. Features, signs, neural checkpoints and classical-control caches were not regenerated.

Merck Form 8937 and SEC distribution documents were saved with hashes and inspected. The bounded raw OGN SIP request returned 842 records on June 3–4, 2021; only scheduled regular-session observations enter execution. A raw MRK boundary request corroborated opening units and recorded provider-vintage volume/close differences without overwriting originals. The May 7 issuer announcement verifies pre-event public knowledge for the predeclared crossing-exclusion stress. No subscription, account, dependency or broker connection was created.

The smallest residual witness is AVGO June 9, 2021 16:33 UTC. A separate exact-minute raw bar request returned only the 16:34 timestamp; the trades endpoint returned 18 records. Seventeen carry odd-lot condition I; the remaining record carries average/derivative conditions. The [official provider FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq#how-are-bars-aggregated) explains excluded trade conditions and bar construction. Raw prints exist, but no valid standard bar opening price exists in these retrieved sources for the required minute. Taking the next minute would violate timing; adopting a first-odd-lot construction would introduce a new execution-data convention. Neither is silently done.

Ordinary action evidence is unchanged: 267 cash events (16 supported AAPL events, 251 observed uncorroborated), three splits, eight verified-zero security-years and eight unknown AMD/AMZN security-years. These are distinct confidence populations. Unknown years remain zero recorded ordinary events in the base; yield overlays are standalone sensitivity adjustments. No bars or trades were used to upgrade dividend confidence. Broker-specific MRK CIL payments remain conditional despite verified distribution terms.

No 2025+ market partition or API price range was accessed. Calendar geometry and known 2025 payment metadata for 2024 entitlements are separate. New market payloads and diagnostic journals are local research artifacts subject to original provider rights, not an assertion of redistribution permission. Reproduction needs the authorized original archive and the recorded input bytes; provider revisions can legitimately change a new download, in which case release hashes must fail rather than silently update.
