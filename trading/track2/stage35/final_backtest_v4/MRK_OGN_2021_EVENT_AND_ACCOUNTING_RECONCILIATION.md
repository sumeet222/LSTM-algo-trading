# MRK/OGN event and accounting reconciliation

The [Merck Form 8937](https://www.merck.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/124/2021/06/Form-8937.pdf), attachment pages 3–4, and [Organon SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1821825/000119312521180652/d49288d8k.htm) were retrieved successfully, saved byte-for-byte and hashed. Native PDFKit extraction and a rendered page inspection confirmed the attachment. The record date was May 17, distribution June 2, and regular OGN trading began June 3. Regular-way MRK trades through the June 2 close carried distribution rights. The economic adapter therefore uses signed MRK inventory crossing the June 3 ex transition, not a May 17 ownership cutoff. This models regular-way entitlement, not verified broker clearing/due-bill settlement.

The distribution ratio is 0.1 OGN per MRK. Signed 100/-100 MRK produce +10/-10 whole OGN assets/delivery obligations; +4/-4 produce +0.4/-0.4 fractional claims. Aggregate account entitlement is rounded toward zero once; virtual allocations reconcile to account whole shares plus the signed fractional claim. Offsetting virtual entitlements cannot create physical assets or net cash. Gross virtual claims remain visible.

The issuer describes agent aggregation and sale of fractions. Exact broker CIL prices/pay dates are not observed. Base CIL uses the first actually received raw OGN opening reference and pays after five XNYS sessions (June 10); one/ten sessions give June 4/17. Alternative $33.30/$40.70 references cannot be used before the June 3 close plus receipt. These are explicit conditional settlements, not factual remittances.

The bounded Alpaca request returned 842 raw minute bars for June 3–4 with no further page. The first raw open is $34.20 at 13:30 UTC, available after completion and the scenario lag. It is not a price known at the opening decision. Whole OGN is automatically disposed at a later eligible raw-open proxy after observation, subject to prior-volume capacity and external costs. New discretionary risk remains blocked while the special claim is unpriced or whole disposal is pending. Missing prices retain signed obligations.

Form 8937’s illustrative June 3 closing values are MRK $73.91 and OGN $37.00. Its illustrative 95.23%/4.77% allocation is long tax-basis metadata only. It does not mark the morning asset, set a trade price or change aggregate equity. The cached last regular minute close can differ from an official auction close. No exact market-price drop decomposition is imposed. A fabricated constant-equity decomposition independently tests conservation.

`MRK_RAW_BOUNDARY_WITNESS_V4.json` contains original June 2/3 opening and final-minute observations, without returns or strategy P&L. `MRK_RAW_BASIS_COMPARISON_V4.json` compares those to a fresh raw provider response: opening prices agree; volumes and one close differ by vendor vintage. The frozen original MRK source is retained. No new model data or original action facts are changed.

Exact source, payload hashes, timing and scenario terms are in `MRK_OGN_SOURCE_AND_ACCOUNTING_MANIFEST_V4.json` and the saved source receipts. Actual financial readiness still depends on the complete engine/pilot gates; source acquisition alone does not clear them.
