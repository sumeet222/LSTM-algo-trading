# Ex-post settlement and internal transfers

Version1's contemporaneous transfer-price restriction remains preserved. V2 accepts instructions using only pre-event information, nets them by account/arm/ticker/minute, and settles after the bar is completed and received. Request objects are immutable. An unknown future open never changes their quantity, submission time, pretrade eligibility or borrow check.

For signed requests d_c, Q=sum(d_c). Internally match stable cohort IDs at one **observed raw bar open**, after receipt. Internal signed shares and cash sum to zero, with zero external fees/spread. Externally fill only the feasible net Q, constrained by pretrade prior-volume capacity. Stable pro-rata residual allocation conserves exact decimal fees and quantities. The external modeled price is raw open*(1+sign(Q)*.001) at baseline. Commission is max($1,.005*cumulative external shares), charged incrementally across partials of the same external order ID. No net external quantity means no external commission.

An observed adverse open is booked, including cash/NAV/cap breaches. No retrospective rejection. Emergency funding, if needed, increases cash only with a matching explicitly attributed liability; it blocks new risk. Stable subsequent liquidation requests require new submission and subsequent valid data; they are not guaranteed fills. Missing/halted/invalid/revised bars retain reservations/inventory and UNRESOLVED status. Late corrections require explicit replay, never silent replacement of old journal prices.

Separate times: decision_at <= submitted_at < effective_fill_at=bar_start; observed_bar_complete_at=start+1m <= bar_available_at <= book_posted_at. Test fixtures often receive at start+2m; that is a fabricated receipt scenario, not measured historical feed timing. An actual source loader must supply its explicitly declared bar receipt policy. Input hourly receipt+2 and opening-reference completion are separately documented in the frozen signal layer.

Ordinary 10:30ET issuance is10:32, first possible entry marker10:33. Endpoint14:30, exit decision no earlier than14:32, subsequent marker14:33. Markers do not guarantee received volume, short availability, valid bars, funding or execution. Overnight and early-close decisions use the XNYS next eligible minute. Entries expire nominal+10; exits remain outstanding/unresolved if unexecutable, with carry.

Physical cash C includes restricted proceeds R exactly once. `NAV=C+sum(q_physical*raw_mark)+sum(cohort_accruals)`. Spendable cash is C-R. Virtual gross and pending reservations remain separate even if physical net is zero. `q_physical=sum(q_virtual)` and `C-initial_cash=sum(cohort_cash)`. Cohort cash+basis-realized-nontrade_cash and physical cash+basis-realized-nontrade_cash reconcile. Gross/name/vintage caps use all virtual claims, not just physical net. Borrow charges are allocated once among virtual shorts in proportion to their absolute shares, using physical net-short notional at the previous known mark over each elapsed ACT/365 segment.

Golden witness and tests use fabricated prices exclusively. The historical source shadow has no funded account, physical orders, fills, cash or P&L. Internal transfer prices are attribution conventions, not observed individual exchange fills or arbitrage.

Known capped partials leave unsubmitted target remainder underthis discrete-minute proxy;expiry cancels that remainder only. Unknown execution/resting status is not a known cancellation. Expiry runs at explicit timer events,after receipt batches andbefore new-vintage/due-exit decisions. A funding deficit has per-cohort claims andnew-riskHOLD untilrepaid;repayment clears the liability once,without changing NAV.
