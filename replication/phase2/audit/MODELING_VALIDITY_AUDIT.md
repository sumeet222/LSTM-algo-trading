## Classification rules

Every concern below receives one of these exact classifications:

- **CONFIRMED DEFECT**: a demonstrated timing or implementation error. Its disposition says whether it was corrected before fitting or deliberately retained as an accepted historical defect.
- **RULED OUT UNDER THIS IMPLEMENTATION**: the identified failure mode is excluded by the inspected implementation and cited checks, within their stated synthetic or admitted-data scope. This is not a universal absence-of-leakage claim.
- **UNVERIFIED LIVE ASSUMPTION**: the historical availability, arrival, or operational assumption is not proved by the available records.
- **INHERITED DATA LIMITATION**: the authorized input contract retains an upstream dependency that was not independently rebuilt or authenticated in full.
- **ACCEPTED HISTORICAL DESIGN LIMITATION**: a deliberate accepted protocol or delivery-scope restriction, preserved rather than changed after observing results.
