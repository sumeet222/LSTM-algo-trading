## 9. Ordinary limitations and final review disposition

One seed, four repeatedly inspected years, overlapping horizons, a fixed survivor panel, a source with substantial missingness, and retrospective vendor/action curation limit interpretation even if every arithmetic check succeeds. Aux missingness is concentrated in particular stocks/periods; source audit does not establish missing-at-random. Added heads change RNG consumption/training trajectory and selected epochs as well as adding supervision, so an apparent gain is not an isolated causal decomposition effect. C3 can improve MSE while changing MAE/conditional/tail performance. All original numerical results supporting or weakening those claims remain sealed.

Phase 1 has **not** performed retraining validation, source rebuild, leakage perturbation, gradient checking, calibration or OOS scoring. Every proposed check above remains future work requiring explicit Phase 2 authorization. Human review should resolve Q01–Q06 and the \* timing acceptance before transferring the clean-room bundle. No repair, scientific promotion or continuation is implied.
