# 05 — Ranking-first contract v0.1

**PROPOSAL — NOT HUMAN-FROZEN. No training objective implemented or fitted.**

The reference remains the original four-hour TG20 price-only interval and frozen HF20 population. Subsequent post-issuance endpoints, extra origins, universe changes and new horizons are separate decisions. Preserve current results under their original semantics.

1. **Outcome/order.** Rank subsequent same-interval stock log returns within an eligible origin. Current R, TG20 and asinh(TG20/s) have identical realized ordering because their centering/positive denominator/positive scale are common to the origin and asinh is strictly increasing. Stock-specific risk scaling changes ordering. Monotonic inverse preserves a saved prediction vector's ordering, not equality between conditional transformed and original means.
2. **Primary evaluation.** Equal-session mean of within-origin Spearman; equal eligible origins within each session; average ranks for ties, constant-vector IC undefined and constant rate reported. Publish unweighted origin IC separately. Secondary pairwise concordance, normalized rank error, tie-aware top/bottom4 retrieval, score margins/dispersion and date stability. An uncalibrated rank score has no justified original-return MSE or automatic sinh inverse.
3. **Finite loss alternatives.** (A) q=2(midrank−1)/(N−1)−1 normalized-rank squared regression: estimates conditional expected normalized rank under squared loss; bounded label, no raw-return magnitude multiplier, but network gradients need not be bounded. (B) Pure mean valid-unordered-pair softplus(−sign(Ri−Rj)(si−sj)/tau), proposed tau1 pending approval: equal-session/origin normalization; omit exact realized ties, half-credit score ties in evaluation. Score-difference derivative bounded1/tau; parameter gradient not globally bounded. This is a pure ranking comparison, unlike R1's z-MSE+0.5pair auxiliary term. Scalar-score preferences cannot encode every nontransitive conditional pair-probability relation.
4. **Simple comparator.** One regularized linear score receiving the same48×16history and same allowed context, with train-only scaling/penalty selection inside each earlier cutoff. No fit is authorized here. A latest-step linear control must be labeled a smaller information set. Do not default to a five-loss/neural/tree sweep; listwise or differentiable-ranking follow-up needs an identified surrogate limitation and separate approval.
5. **Selection proposal.** Ranking-led daily validation IC, initial best−infinity, strictly absolute improvement greater than0.001IC units, deterministic earlier-epoch ties, min12/max60epochs and patience10 as a reviewable starting proposal. Absolute improvement works across negative/zero IC; do not mechanically apply0.999×loss to signed IC. Exact monitor/minimum/patience/threshold remain UNFROZEN. No smoothing or alternative selector was applied to prior models.
6. **Chronology proposal.** At least three nonoverlapping outer chronological periods selected by dates before fitting; each uses earlier matured labels, inner selection and independently fitted transforms, matched comparator/treatment populations and seeds. Exact folds, material-effect rule and neural-start cap require approval. Date-block sensitivity preserves whole cross-sections;190pairs/origin are not independent dates. Never relabel in-sample refit-by-year as walk-forward OOS. Retrospective folds after studying2025 remain robustness evidence, not pristine prospective confirmation.
7. **Window policy proposal.** Compare one fixed-length recent window with expanding history only if era evidence motivates it; exact length/cadence remain UNFROZEN. Independently initialized fits and warm-started updates are different interventions. Stitch only predictions actually made after a permitted earlier cutoff; all losses/control feedback must have matured. A rolling48step feature window is neither fitting policy.
8. **Diagnostics.** Preserve original outcomes and tail/numerator/MAD/source cohorts. Rank regression has pointwise loss allocation; pairwise diagnostic allocation gives each endpoint half of each normalized pair loss and must sum to actual optimized loss. No fabricated TG20 error for rank-only scores. Preserve score ties/margins, cohort concordance and temporal concentration. A future minute or multi-horizon model needs its own source masks, exact endpoint and per-horizon maturity/validity/weights. Do not silently require every horizon valid for all heads.
9. **Magnitude and economics.** E1 probability, QR1 quantiles and absolute/path magnitude are separate outcomes. A magnitude forecast alone supplies no general directional advantage. Any stacking needs historical out-of-fold/prequential magnitude predictions, not optimistic same-label fitted outputs. No portfolio, P&L, cost threshold, horizon winner or score-amplitude optimization now.
