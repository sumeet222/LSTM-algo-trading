2.1 A4 — Leave-one-out peer mean and dispersion of historical M1
For each origin t, stock i and input step s∈{1,…,48}, let u_{j,t,s} be channel M1 from the exact loaded 16-channel, phase-correct scaled input of peer j, including its existing fitted fallback/quality behavior (do not invent a price or separate scaler):
mu_peer[i,t,s] = (1/19) * sum_{j != i} u[j,t,s]
sd_peer[i,t,s] = sqrt((1/19) * sum_{j != i} (u[j,t,s] - mu_peer[i,t,s])**2)
x_peer[i,t,s]  = concat(x_FT16[i,t,s], [mu_peer[i,t,s], sd_peer[i,t,s]])
Fixed population divisor 19, not 18 or sample-variance divisor; no epsilon needed for nonnegative variance except a numerical clamp of roundoff-negative variance to zero (document). Tensor [B,20,48,18]; ordinary shared hidden32 LSTM and unchanged scalar head. A4 leaves each target's own 16 FT16 channels unchanged and adds precisely 2 computed peer channels. Frozen M1 is a designated feature channel, not a raw return, realized TG20 future and not volume quality. Old D8 five-stock formula with denominator4 is only the precedent; this is its exact N=20 generalization.
Check analytically against loops and an independent NumPy implementation; test zero peer self-inclusion, peer perturbation effects, and stationarity under canonical stock permutation (after mapping ticker identities). In this strictly controlled architecture no sector pool, directed H↔L pair, mean of target labels, peer momentum on future candles, data-driven ticker selection, or other summary/horizon. Expected parameter count if original T2 state matches 6,433: 6,689 (+4×hidden32×2=256); verify actual parameter-group count, do not force counts to match with hidden extra features. 2.2 A5 — SP3 rank-4, stock-to-market-to-stock mixing
Encode all twenty separately but simultaneously through the exact base stockwise TM0/T2 LSTM to get H∈R[B,20,32]. Apply non-affine LayerNorm across each stock's 32 hidden coordinates. With fixed bottleneck rank K=4 for N=20, bias-free learned matrices M1∈R[4,20], M2∈R[20,4]:
\[
\boxed{\widetilde H=H+M_2\,\mathrm{GELU}\!\left(M_1\,\mathrm{LN}(H)\right),\qquad
\hat z_i=\mathrm{Head}(\widetilde h_i).}
\]
Interpret M1 as operating along the stock axis, with the feature dimension32 processed independently for each bottleneck coordinate; M2 projects back to the 20 fixed stock positions. No pooling over prediction origins or sessions; no mean of future targets. Preserve 32-dimensional hidden channels and scalar head. Init M1 from a predeclared deterministic small seeded nondegenerate matrix rule; initialize M2=0 for exact residual identity to the same model instance's TM0 encoder on all inputs. M1 gradient can initially be zero by construction, but confirm both matrices can receive gradients on a planted peer-signal task and inspect eventual actual fit gradients. Rank4 is a new fixed N=20 adaptation of the older N5 rank2 design, NOT a claim that rank4 is optimal; absolutely no rank/hyperparameter sweep. No bias, separate dropout, LayerNorm affine parameters, stock embeddings, sector features, cross-stock temporal attention or learned market state.
Expected additive trainable parameters 4×20+20×4=160 → 6,593 total if T2 base remains6,433; verify code's actual count and size. Identity/order dependence: ticker-axis weights are position-specific. Under a simultaneous permutation P of ticker coordinates, an equivalent representation must use M1'=M1 P^T, M2'=P M2 and appropriately permute targets; reproduce output equivariance numerically with this coordinate-consistent transformation. Randomly permuting input tickers without permuting weights is not supposed to be invariant and is not a model bug. Test that a genuine change in peer hidden vectors can affect another ticker's output once the mixer is nonzero; quantify fitted off-diagonal dependence so an apparently successful SP3 module isn't merely a diagonal stock-ID adjustment. No economic causal-influence inference from matrix entries.
2.3 A6 — SP1 one-head cross-stock attention over final LSTM hidden states
Use the same original TM0/T2 stockwise LSTM to form H∈R[B,20,32]. Apply LN(H) over hidden coordinates with no affine weights, and one attention head of key/value dimension16 with bias-free matrices WQ,WK,WV∈R[32,16], WO∈R[16,32]:
\[
Q=\mathrm{LN}(H)W_Q,\quad K=\mathrm{LN}(H)W_K,\quad V=\mathrm{LN}(H)W_V,\quad
A=\operatorname{rowSoftmax}\left(QK^\top/\sqrt{16}\right),
\]
\[
\boxed{\widetilde H=H+AVW_O,\qquad \hat z_i=\mathrm{Head}(\widetilde h_i).}
\]
The 20×20 softmax attends to all stocks including each stock's own key/value, consistent with legacy SP1; report diagonal self-attention vs off-diagonal peer mass separately. Mixing is within an origin only. Initialize WO=0 exactly to preserve the parent readout at initialization; initialize the three input projections from a fixed seeded rule. Initial WQ/WK/WV gradients can be zero until WO changes; verify eventual computational/fit signal and verify nonzero peer Jacobians on the planted task. No learned ticker positional embedding, additional head, forecast-origin-target mask, special dropout, value-gate, regime features, or full temporal-time cross-stock attention. Add 2,048 parameters → expected 8,481 total if base has6,433. Mathematically, if all rows of H and outputs are permuted by any P on both input and output axes, SP1 is permutation-equivariant in eval mode; test this with corresponding labels, and test stock-order protection in the loader. Attention weights are dynamic associations, not causal peer influence explanations.
