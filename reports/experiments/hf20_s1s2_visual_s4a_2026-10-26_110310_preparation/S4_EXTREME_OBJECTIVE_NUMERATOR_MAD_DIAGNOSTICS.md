# S4-A detailed extreme-value, objective-error and numerator/MAD diagnostics

All eight runs and all four roles are retained below. TRAIN_EVENT95/99 thresholds come from the appropriate training prefix. TARGET_RANK_TOP1/5 describe realized absolute TG20 tails within the evaluated role; ACTUAL_OBJECTIVE_TOP1/5 separately rank the optimized row error. These post-outcome cohorts are diagnostic, not available forecast regimes. Complements and positive/negative subsets remain explicit.

Contribution is a population-weighted sum, not a conditional mean. Loss share divides that contribution by the same run/role ALL objective. NUM_ONLY, MAD_ONLY, BOTH and NEITHER form a partition; FLOOR can overlap. Subsets from different families must not be added. NR has no invented return inverse or original-Y MSE. T2R original-Y diagnostics invert the frozen recipe once. Energy terms below are in original TG20 squared units, separate from optimized asinh loss. Subset common/centered decompositions require their cross term. Counts reused across models are not independent source events.
