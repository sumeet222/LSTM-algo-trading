Canonical formula (do not substitute the earlier five-stock TG0, historical volatility, or a stock-specific MAD):
r_i,t = log(P_i,t+4h / P_i,t) using exact genuine approved same-session source-close endpoints;
m_t = median_j r_j,t over the 20 eligible names;
n_i,t = r_i,t − m_t (signed numerator);
MAD_t = median_j |r_j,t − m_t| (raw cross-sectional denominator base);
