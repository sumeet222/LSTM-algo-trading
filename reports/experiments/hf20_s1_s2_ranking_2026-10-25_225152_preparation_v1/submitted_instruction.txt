2. Statistical contract and recommended numerical defaults (freeze before fitting)
Target and score
At permitted origin t, current target is the existing
\[
Y_{i,t}=\frac{R^{(4)}_{i,t}-\operatorname{median}_jR^{(4)}_{j,t}}{\operatorname{MAD}_j(R^{(4)}_{j,t})+\epsilon_{\mathrm{fit}}},
\quad R^{(4)}_{i,t}=\log(P_{i,e(t)}/P_{i,b(t)}).
\]
Because the median and positive MAD denominator are common across all 20 stocks at a given origin, the within-origin rankings of raw future four-hour return, TG20, and its monotonically transformed asinh target coincide. Reconstruct independently with original P020 timestamps, eligibility and recipes. Never use the future cross-sectional median/MAD as an input.

For ascending observed average/midranks r_i ∈ [1,20], normalized training labels are
\[
q_{i,t}=2\frac{r_{i,t}-1}{19}-1,\qquad
L_{\mathrm{NR},t}=\frac1{20}\sum_{i=1}^{20}(s_{i,t}-q_{i,t})^2.
\]
Use exactly the original 20 tickers in canonical order. Midranks/ties deterministic; do not inject jitter. Scores s_i have ranking units, not predicted TG20 or return units. Unless a separate, historically fitted calibration is explicitly approved, sinh(s_i), original-target MSE, annualized returns or Sharpe ratios of a rank-only score are scientifically invalid.

For distinct-outcome unordered pairs (i<j) define d_ij = sign(R_i-R_j) and
\[
L_{\mathrm{PW},t}=\frac1{|\mathcal P_t|}\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal P_t}
\operatorname{softplus}\big[-d_{ij}(s_i-s_j)/\tau\big].
\]
Propose/freeze τ=1.0 unless real-data-free numerical tests expose a correctness issue. With complete untied 20-stock observations there are 190 unordered pairs per origin. Exclude true tied-outcome pairs under one deterministic rule, report pair coverage. Never count pairs as independent market events or increase an origin's statistical weight because it has 190 pairs. All objectives use equal stocks/pairs → permitted origins within session → sessions, with origin/session averaging matching existing HF20 conventions.

0 — preflight	Repo/source hashes; real folds/cutoffs/maturity; access guard; journal/budget/authority card	Protected-I/O risk, source mismatch, noncausal fold, missing required records
1 — rehearsal	Synthetic independent oracles; rank/ties/weights/pairs; correct loss/gradient; concise code/static test receipt	Wrong rank direction, weight normalization, pair-loss allocation, state leakage
2 — S1 fits	Matched four-fold baseline predictions, selected penalty, exact OOS keys and report	Unapproved fits, outer-year tuning, data leak, irreproducible output
3 — S2 fits	Exact enumerated neural starts, selector traces, calibration/refit isolation, OOS predictions	Unapproved start, nonfinite/invalid gradients, wrong clock or checkpoint
4 — diagnostics	Rank and original-target tail accounting; truthful loss units; per-year comparisons and case traces	Fabricated MSE, loss nonconservation, incomparable dates or leakage
5 — closeout	Ledger/preservation/protection receipt; concise reports; no unrun PASS claims	Broken source provenance, unexplained checksum or ledger divergence


Do not fail Stage 2 or 3 solely because Rank IC is low, negative, or similar to a constant profile. Technically valid negative results are the point of a controlled scientific experiment. A true technical or authorization blocker stops subsequent starts; publish the partial results rather than quietly repairing, retraining or changing the question.
