## S3 endpoint-eligibility amendment — audit before training

The reported loss of approximately 25% of fitting origins and reduction of F24 inner validation from 74 to 42 sessions is a material concern.

**Do not lower the 50-session validation minimum yet.**

Instead, revise the proposed S3 eligibility design as follows:

1. **Horizon-specific eligibility:** For S3-A, construct independent valid-origin sets for the 1-, 2-, and 4-hour targets. A valid 1-hour observation must not be excluded solely because its 4-hour endpoint is unavailable. Preserve the original FT16 input and source-quality requirements.
2. **Matched horizon comparison:** Also construct the intersection of eligible origins across all three horizons for a secondary, strictly matched comparison. Do not use this intersection as the mandatory fitting population unless scientifically justified.
3. **S3-B matching:** Within each horizon, require the original-M2 and horizon-aligned-M2 models to use identical origin/stock populations. The input-feature comparison must not be confounded by sample selection.
4. **Validation support:** Retain the existing 50-session minimum initially. Report the actual fitting, validation, refit and OOS counts for every horizon, year and experiment arm. Include retention percentages, origin-slot composition and reasons for exclusions.
5. **Historical comparability:** Preserve the existing four-hour TG20 reference and archived S1/S2 outcomes. Clearly distinguish newly constructed horizon-specific populations from the original four-hour population. Do not silently alter source clocks, target endpoints, embargoes or label-maturity rules.
6. **Readiness decision:** If horizon-specific eligibility restores sufficient support, propose the updated design-freeze card and continue only with authorized non-training preparation. If any fold still fails the minimum, report the exact deficiency and propose the smallest defensible alternative for human approval.

Verify endpoint arithmetic, source availability, no leakage, cohort identities and matched-population invariants using inexpensive deterministic tests.

**Stop before any optimizer update.** Do not launch training, relax statistical thresholds, add origins outside the authorized forecast schedule, access protected 2026 numerics, or change the research ledger. Await explicit approval of the amended eligibility design.
