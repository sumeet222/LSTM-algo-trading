3. Quantitative audit B — Why is ZERO competitive? (mandatory exact MSE decomposition)
Using each model's frozen development predictions and the same canonical TG20 values and equal-day weights, independently reproduce:
MSE(C0) ≈ 3.478315, MSE(T2) ≈ 3.475102, MSE(ZERO) ≈ 3.474506 with documented rounding tolerance. Make no claims without matching origin/date/20-stock row keys and the recorded 366-origin mask.
For each model, decompose the difference exactly on this weighted population:
\[
\operatorname{MSE}(\widehat y)-\operatorname{MSE}(0)
=\mathbb{E}_w[\widehat y^2]-2\mathbb{E}_w[y\widehat y],
\]
where E_w respects 20 equal stock weights within each eligible origin, equal eligible origins within each day, equal days, not a naive row-weighted mean if day/origin counts differ. Report the numerical components E_w[y²], E_w[ŷ²], E_w[yŷ], improvement fraction and check their algebra to float precision. Decompose errors also by origin 1/2, stock, month and requested TG20-magnitude bins. Distinguish effects of model mean/bias, forecast variance, slope/calibration and target conditional noise.
Diagnostics:
- Prediction mean/SD/range/quantiles, original TG20 mean/SD/quantiles, positive/negative fractions, within-origin cross-sectional score dispersion versus TG20 dispersion, and how close outputs are to zero; distribution of absolute forecasts vs absolute targets.
- ŷ–y Pearson and Spearman on clearly defined populations (pooled correlations are not substitutes for equal-day per-origin Rank IC). Correlation, covariance, score sign and per-origin cross-sectional forecast rank; ties/nonconstant checks.
- Optional post-hoc diagnostic linear slope/calibration curve E[y | score-bin] using fixed, clearly labeled diagnostic bins (not a trained deployable regressor or fitted new predictor). If producing line fits for display, label diagnostic descriptive and not a new predictive baseline. No selection using development results.
- Separation of a model that learns very small but meaningful relative ranks from one that merely outputs random small fluctuations; show paired Rank IC, concordance and no-signal ZERO distinction (ZERO defined-only Spearman is undefined, project zero is a convention only).
- Check whether target E_w[y] or the average cross-sectional mean is nonzero despite median-centering. Within-origin median is centered, but cross-sectional mean need not be zero; do not confuse them.
Deliverable B: mse_vs_zero_exact_decomposition.csv, forecast_dispersion_and_calibration.csv, stock_origin_month_diagnostics.csv, and a short narrative separating MSE weakness from possible rank signal.
