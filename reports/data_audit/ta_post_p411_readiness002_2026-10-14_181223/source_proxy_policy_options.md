# Source/proxy policy options v1 — proposals only

All options below are **RESEARCHER_PROXY_PROPOSED**, except the unchanged requirement to use independently supported historical facts. No option is accepted, installed as DOCUMENTED, or used to construct a real feature/target/scaler.

| Option | Concrete use and limits | Required authority / gate effect |
|---|---|---|

Latency example: a 10:30-completed step under a 30-second assumption is available at 10:30:30. It is available by a hypothetical 10:32 issue but **fails the frozen 10:30 own-step cutoff**. Delaying issuance does not authorize incorporating a later-ended bar or relabeling publication. Every positive latency in this table rejects the latest step under the unchanged cutoff rule; assumed zero latency supplies at most the calendar/key bound, not source certification. At 301 seconds it also misses the maximum 5-minute issuance allowance. Training/WF1/WF2 bounds are 1502/248/248; the CSV keeps actual versus hypothetical fields explicit.

Falsification controls: any documented source release later than an assumed time, revisions not available then, incompatible action epoch, incomplete minute/reference population, or incorrect source instrument defeats the proposed schedule for that row. A successful hypothetical test cannot falsify UNKNOWN into truth. No latency is chosen by returns/retention/performance; own-step rules apply at every historical location and to fitted-certificate availability.

A future own-step rule tied to issue time rather than original cutoff would be a material clock/certificate change, not an operational fix. It requires explicit protocol review and affected source/feature/maturity/retention recomputation. No such rule is adopted here.
