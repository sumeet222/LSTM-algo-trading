6. New 20-stock target, evaluation clocks and labels
Create an additive TG20_HF20_CROSS_SECTIONAL_V1 preserving the scientific intent of the old TG0—relative future stock-specific movements—while explicitly versioning the 20-name cross-section and new hourly clock.
For a candidate origin t and each of 20 stocks with genuine compatible four-trading-hour price-only proxy endpoints:
r_i,t = log(C_i,exit / C_i,entry)     # verified adjusted price units, future/outcome
m_t   = median_{j in U20}(r_j,t)
a_t   = median_{j in U20} |r_j,t - m_t|      # unscaled cross-sectional MAD
eps   = max(1e-4, 0.1 * median_{t in permitted TRAIN} a_t)
TG20_i,t = (r_i,t - m_t) / (a_t + eps)
