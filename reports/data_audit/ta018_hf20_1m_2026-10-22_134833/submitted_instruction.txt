3. Causal missing-minute filling — only after minute QA PASS
User explicitly requested forward-fill missing minute candles before forming hourly candles. Implement it conservatively and reproducibly:
1. Construct the authentic exchange-calendar scheduled minute-start grid for regular sessions, with 390 regular minutes on normal sessions and calendar-correct early closes, for each ticker/day.
2. Preserve untouched source_open/high/low/close/volume and is_genuine at the source/raw layer. In a separate derived minute view, where a bounded, otherwise QA-approved scheduled minute is absent, use the latest earlier genuine or already causally carried close from the same stock and same session: synthetic open=high=low=close=prior_close, volume=0, and is_imputed=True plus fill_run_length, source provenance, and reason. This creates an unchanged-price/no-transaction bar, not a real market trade or reliable executable price.
3. Never fill backward, never use future observations to repair an earlier bar, never forward-fill across overnight, weekend, holiday or exchange-open boundary; no cross-ticker filling; no filling from future bar open/high/low. Missing first-minute/opening reference or absent initial in-session close remains ineligible rather than borrowed from previous day's close. No filling invalid observed bars automatically; those must be adjudicated in raw QA.
4. Only small gap lengths pre-approved by the repository's data QA policy may be imputed. If no such limit exists, use a conservative maximum 2 consecutive missing minutes as a provisional guard, and STOP for human discussion if longer runs or a meaningful percentage of required prediction-window bars would otherwise require filling. Preserve gap metrics even if an exceptionally sparse day would be excluded rather than filled.
5. Preserve is_genuine, is_imputed, full source timestamps, filled-minute count, longest fill run and optional first/last source minute in all downstream aggregates. Filled minutes never turn a low-quality source month into a passing month, nor count as genuine execution-price prints.
6. Verify monotonic causality: a feature calculated at minute t cannot change when a future minute t+1 is perturbed; the source/raw data remains byte-identical to the recorded version.
