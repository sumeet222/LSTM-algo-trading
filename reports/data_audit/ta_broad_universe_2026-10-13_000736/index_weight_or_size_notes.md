# Index influence versus liquidity

All29 formation index weights and contemporaneous market-cap-size proxies are
NOT_MEASURED. No panel min/max/median/max:min/AAPL:other-median/dispersion is
reported without genuine dated weights. Current2026 observations are not carried
back to2020. Public source searches did not authenticate a complete2020-07-01
29-name constituent/free-float/sector snapshot. A current factsheet or a dated
partial index-change announcement cannot verify the full universe. No fees or
licenses were acquired; optional weight evidence does not stall the source screen.

S&P's official [index description](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/)
identifies float-adjusted market-cap weighting. Constituent influence is
FMC_i / sum(FMC_j) over genuine constituents at that date. The separate D1
liquidity proxy is mean(raw vendor daily close × share volume) over60 sessions.
Trading volume does not determine index weight. Five TG0 rank positions and the
nominal equal-slot exposure policy do not inherit those constituent weights.
AAPL inclusion in SPY can produce contemporaneous coupling; benchmark inclusion
alone does not establish future-information leakage. No U1–U5, target, SPY source,
portfolio exposure rule, benchmark or hard weight-balance cap changes here.
