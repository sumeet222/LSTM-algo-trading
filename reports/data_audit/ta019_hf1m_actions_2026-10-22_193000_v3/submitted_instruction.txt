0. Purpose and operating philosophy
We have successfully acquired Package 018D's July 2010–March 2026 historical source. The user's preference is explicit: proceed with the data we have unless one or two stocks have genuinely egregious/unrepairable problems or the resulting training sample is demonstrably unusable. Ordinary scattered missing minute candles, modest incompleteness, and ambiguous duplicates are not reasons to discard an entire year, stock, or training plan. At the same time, do not quietly invent returns/volume or train on unadjusted stock-split artifacts. First investigate and conclusively account for every material price discontinuity against reliable public evidence; if any material jump remains unresolved, stop before feature construction or training. Then perform a fast, quantitative readiness assessment and—if acceptable—build the dataset, train matched C0 and T2 LSTMs, save comprehensive analysis artifacts, and stop. Prefer focused tests over long full-suite runs.
Source evidence from 018D to confirm locally (do not assume these numbers without checking manifest): ~36,462,461 selected raw minute rows; 279,180 absent expected RTH stock-minute slots; 406 conflicting ticker/minute keys; 226,423 bounded minute price-only proxies under cap five; 552,500 hourly/stub bins; 532 bins with no genuine minute; 13 unverified scale/action jumps. About 76.72% of full-hour timestamps across all 20 stocks satisfy the earlier bounded-price proxy standard (which is not yet a valid-sequence or training-rate measurement). Requiring 100% genuine minutes across all 20 would retain only ~11.70% and is not the intended training rule. We expect to keep many valid observations using explicit missingness handling and label masks.
Primary success condition: a documented PASS_ACTION_VERIFICATION gate covering the 13 known candidate jumps and any additional material jumps found in a deterministic full-panel scan; then one source-isolated, versioned, auditable new training panel plus a matched seed-42 C0 versus T2 experiment, only if the derived 48-step input sequences and reliable targets are sufficiently numerous. Rare isolated minute gaps should not stop the run, but unexplained material price jumps must stop it, because they can contaminate returns, long-lookback features, and target normalization.
