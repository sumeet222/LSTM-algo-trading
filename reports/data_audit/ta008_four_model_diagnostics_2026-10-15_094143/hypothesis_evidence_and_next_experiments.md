**NO_HYPERPARAMETER_CHANGE_YET.** No deterministic defect was found in the checked candidate path. Distributional concentration is established; clipping causes, temporal stability and architecture limits remain unresolved.

1. **Large TG0 / low MAD concentrates MSE: SUPPORTED.** Confidence: High for distribution; unresolved for clipping causality. Evidence: Targets reach 82.03; the worst 10% of training sessions contribute about 55.6% of loss. Both large relative numerators and small denominators are present. Limits: There are no zero MADs; floor dominance occurs in only 1.33% of refit origins. Batch-gradient linkage was not retained. Sources: target_outlier_hypotheses.md; target_loss_overlap.json. Next check: Independently inspect the named retrospective price references if needed; retain current labels.

2. **No comparative advantage on this seed/fold: SUPPORTED.** Confidence: High descriptively, low for generalization. Evidence: ZERO has the lowest MSE. E5−TM0 mean IC is only +0.0038793. Limits: Ranks and MSE measure different objectives; five-name observations and within-day origins are dependent. Sources: model_comparison_and_uncertainty.md. Next check: Choose one separately approved unchanged-model replication.

3. **Optimization instability after an early useful epoch: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED.** Confidence: Moderate. Evidence: Late clipping and validation deterioration occur in all four trajectories. E5 retains warned refit epochs. Limits: Early selected TM0/MC0/MC1 states precede the worst late updates. Clipping can be protective; overshoot, tail noise and recurrent amplification are not separated. Sources: g6_four_model_epochs.csv; epoch_associations.json. Next check: Add compact batch loss/session-hash/preclip-norm telemetry only to a later approved fit.

4. **Overfitting or nonstationarity: UNRESOLVED.** Confidence: Low. Evidence: Validation deteriorates later and monthly contrasts reverse. Limits: Final-prefix training MSE exceeds inner-validation MSE, and populations, floors and modes differ. This is not a controlled overfitting gap; no second seed/fold exists. Sources: monthly_paired_deltas.csv; loss_concentration.csv. Next check: Review shifts, then consider a separately approved WF2 replication.

5. **Hidden/output collapse or gate saturation: NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE.** Confidence: High within the checked scope. Evidence: Small MC0/MC1 score spread warranted inspection. Limits: Scores are finite, nonconstant and tie-free; sampled hidden states vary. Gate weights vary without saturation and frozen scores replay. Sources: score_health.json; boundary_replay_compact.json; gate_sample_summary.json. Next check: No rescue is justified by a collapse claim; preserve the checks.

6. **Market/peer increments are absent or unstable: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED.** Confidence: Low to moderate. Evidence: MC0 underperforms TM0 here; MC1 gains cancel; E5 trades a small IC increase against worse MSE. Limits: One seed/fold and different capacities cannot establish absence of useful information or causal peer transmission. Counterfactuals show computational use only. Sources: mechanism_and_score_health.md. Next check: Replicate the primary TM0/MC0 comparison without changing loss, features or timing.

7. **A feature-scaling implementation defect: NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE.** Confidence: Moderate for the audited boundaries. Evidence: Representation and scale mismatch were considered. Limits: Raw-to-scaled endpoints, input prefixes and identities match; distributions are finite. That does not rule out optimization sensitivity to legitimate tails. Sources: input_channel_distribution.csv; boundary_replay_compact.json. Next check: Inspect the largest channel tails before proposing any separate scaling study.
