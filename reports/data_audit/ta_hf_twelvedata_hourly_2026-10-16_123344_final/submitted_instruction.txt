0. Research question and success criteria
We want to know whether the public Hugging Face Twelve Data hourly equity files can provide a new, independently versioned, at-least-20-stock US-equity panel appropriate for future cross-sectional LSTM experiments. Do not treat the dataset's aggregate ~690k hourly rows as proof of seven years per ticker. Measure actual coverage and missingness. We have already screened many Alpaca candidates and encountered missing candles and limits on older history; do not repeat Alpaca candidate searches.
Evaluate two distinct opportunities, explicitly:
- A — strict continuity: at least 20 stocks with sufficiently clean, consistent hourly historical bars across our original preconfirmation research horizon, particularly the training history July 2020–June 2023 and development July 2023–June 2024, plus enough prehistory for 48-hour lookbacks and longer rolling technical indicators. A compatible bar clock, adjustment convention, and scientifically defensible provenance are mandatory before saying STRICT_COMPATIBLE.
- B — revised research chronology: if A fails, identify the longest practical common continuous history for ≥20 names and propose new train/development/untouched-test splits with realistic origin counts; protect the resulting test period from model selection. This is a separate experimental design, not an unannounced modification of the frozen original splits.
No-go outcomes are valid. A highly inconsistent dataset is not improved by pretending a missing hour is present or by smoothing away structural gaps.
1. Known publisher claims — verify, do not assume
The publicly described dataset includes 51 large-cap US equities, nine configurations and separate hourly bars_1h train/validation/test Parquets. As currently listed, the hourly files are approximately 102 MB / 20.7 MB / 37.6 MB (total ~160 MB); repository files and revisions may change. Publisher-defined splits are train through 2023-12-31, validation 2024 calendar year, test 2025 to the latest publication; these vendor splits are not our existing WF1/WF2/confirmation boundaries. Published datetime is tz-aware America/New_York and represents interval START. The card explicitly says intraday history depth varies by ticker and vendor access. close_adj is described as split/dividend-adjusted, but open/high/low/volume adjustment parity and point-in-time corporate-action vintage are not established merely by this description. Verify the actual rows/schema. The dataset has an MIT card label but underlying market data retains Twelve Data redistribution/use restrictions; check applicable terms, label unresolved claims clearly. Check whether credentials are genuinely needed instead of demanding tokens by default.
Official file listing: https://huggingface.co/datasets/twelvedata/financial-world-model/tree/main/bars_1h

3. Exact stock-by-stock data census
For each of the 51 stated symbols actually present, record:
- Symbol, row count in each vendor file and combined, true earliest and latest ET/UTC timestamps, unique sessions and trading dates, duplicate (symbol, timestamp) keys, null and nonfinite OHLCV, bad OHLC inequalities, negative prices or volumes, close_adj missingness and consistency.
- Timestamp hour/minute distribution and explicit interval-start semantics; regular-session-only claims versus observed after-hours, DST offsets, early closes, gaps across market closures, and any suspicious timetable drift. Don't silently equate a 1h vendor bar with our completed hour when the start/end grid disagrees.
- For each exchange date, expected 1h positions derived from an actual XNYS trading calendar, including half-day behavior. Distinguish calendar-standard expected hours, vendor-specific RTH convention and genuine observed keys. Our existing first-pass project uses 30-minute bars, complete hour starts, and a separate closing stub; do not assume a 1h bar gives the original 30-minute constituents, intra-hour high/low/volume/trade-count or exact downstream FT0 channels.
- Number and percentage of genuine complete expected hours, per-year/per-month counts, missing hours, maximum consecutive gaps, missing terminal hours, missing entire sessions, coverage around the original daily 10:30/11:30 ET prediction origins, and exact 4-trading-hour reference endpoints. Quantify calendar-match failures explicitly.
- Stable active range and usable common overlap; detailed strict-2020–2024 coverage and separately best alternative 20-stock overlap. Do not backfill gaps by interpolation for acceptance.
- Check for possible ticker changes/relistings and issuer identity ambiguities; don't claim point-in-time S&P membership or sector classification without date-specific evidence.
Output hourly_symbol_census.csv, hourly_missingness_by_month.csv, hourly_calendar_alignment.csv, hourly_schema_quality.json, per_symbol_coverage.json, and stock_universe_shortlist.md with definitions, numerical denominators and evidence bindings.
