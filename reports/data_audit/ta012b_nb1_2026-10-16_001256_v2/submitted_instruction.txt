2. Freeze the single-factor N-B1 transformation mathematically
For any genuine completed hourly bar of target stock \(i\) at a legal historical step \(u\), retain the existing native B1:
\[
 x_{i,u}=\log\!\left(\frac{H_{i,u}}{L_{i,u}}\right)\ge0
\]
with current genuine source quality and bar-completion/available-time requirements unchanged. Introduce one training-fitted strictly positive reference scale:
\[
 s_{\mathcal T}=\operatorname{median}\{x_{i,u}: (i,u)\in\mathcal T_{B1},\ x_{i,u}>0\}.
\]
Here \(\mathcal T_{B1}\) is the exact canonical pooled training-feature key population that original B1's registered train-only scaler uses for this fold/pass; deduplicate repeated 48-step exposures, preserve legitimate stock/key weights and quality masks. No label-based fitting, future/validation/development values or all-time median. Guard a missing/empty strictly positive population as a hard preprocessing HOLD. If actual positive values are tiny but valid, report their scale; do not invent a tuned floor. The 012A auditor must supply the fitting-key lineage and any weighting convention. Define
\[
 q_{i,u}=\operatorname{asinh}\!\left(x_{i,u}/s_{\mathcal T}\right).
\]
Then fit a new ordinary original-style pooled robust scaler to \(q\), on the same permitted B1 training keys:
\[
 c_{\mathcal T}=\operatorname{median}_{\mathcal T_{B1}}q,\qquad
 a_{\mathcal T}=\operatorname{MAD}_{\mathcal T_{B1}}(q)
\]
and use the original B1 robust scaling denominator/fallback convention, applied to transformed \(q\), with exact recorded floor, constant behavior, types and weights. In pseudocode:
new_B1_scaled(u) = original_robust_scale_rule(
    asinh(native_B1(u) / s_training_only),
    median_q_training_only, MAD_q_training_only, original_B1_floor_policy
)
Do not change the original training-fitted epsilon/fallback rule itself. Package 012A found an unrelated synthetic constant-vector numerical near-zero-SD fallback edge; log it as separate HOLD, not a reason to alter original mathematical behavior or to block this B1 experiment when no current real recipe invokes it. Fail closed if the new data actually trigger an ambiguous fallback.
