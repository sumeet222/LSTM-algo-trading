E. Deterministic reconciliation and causal forward filling — do not falsify raw data
Keep three distinct layers: immutable selected-only RAW, quality-classified CANONICAL_OBSERVED (only clear genuine unique minutes), and DERIVED_FILLED (optional conservative proxies). Do not rewrite RAW, and always retain masks and issue IDs enabling precise reproduction. Use the following defaults unless an already approved stronger causal/data-consistent rule exists; if so, document the difference and its justification.
1. Exact duplicate records: Retain all in RAW; collapse identical ticker/UTC-minute/OHLCV copies to a single canonical observation, recording duplicate multiplicity. Do not double-count volume.
2. Conflicting duplicates: Retain every version in RAW and issue ledger. Never arbitrarily pick the first/last row, average disagreeing prices, add conflicting volumes, or claim the result is genuine. Mark that minute ambiguous_conflict; exclude all conflicting versions from CANONICAL_OBSERVED. Treat it as a gap eligible for a clearly flagged price proxy under the same causal bounded-gap rule below. Preserve the evidence for eventual human investigation. If a deterministic source-provided sequence/priority field is actually verified, propose it in report, but do not silently change this default.
3. Invalid bars: Log raw source and mark unusable; treat their minute as absent in the canonical layer, never repair impossible OHLC by clipping without explicit evidence.
4. Short within-session interior gaps: Within a single ticker and single actual trading session, permit up to 5 consecutive missing/ambiguous regular-session minutes to receive a synthetic price proxy, carrying the last earlier unambiguous/canonical price close: O=H=L=C=previous_close; volume=0. Set is_imputed=1, fill_reason, gap_length, observed_volume_unknown=1. The zero volume is only an imputation placeholder, not a claim of no trading; sum observed volume separately. No use of a future minute/price. Maintain sensitivity tables for 2-minute versus 5-minute cap (without rewriting original observed prices). Do not convert an entire missing hour into artificial flat candles.
5. Session opening gap: Never borrow the previous day's close or another stock to fabricate the day's open; mark opening missing. For hourly OHLC, the first genuine minute open can serve as the first observed open with opening_minutes_missing=true and a lower confidence/invalid-boundary flag; do not claim it is the actual session open. If no genuine minute exists in that hour, keep it missing. Do not backfill from future prices to synthesize missing minute OHLC.
6. Session closing gap / hour endpoint: If a short gap permits last-known-price carry, the derived close must be close_is_genuine=false and considered ineligible as a verified forecast-origin/label endpoint until a subsequent protocol explicitly decides otherwise. For gaps >5 minutes leave them null and the hour partial/unusable; the genuine observations may still be aggregated as partial with flags, never labelled complete. Apply equivalent conservatism when high/low/volume are incomplete.
7. Corporate actions and improbable jumps: Never smooth, cap, adjust or forward-fill across a material apparent split or unexplained scale discontinuity. Preserve boundary evidence, mark action uncertainty and prevent use of unverified cross-boundary returns as model labels in any later build. Source acquisition/aggregation may finish with those flags.
8. Time causality: No cross-session, overnight, cross-ticker or calendar-closure filling. DST and half-days follow the exchange calendar; no imputed pre-IPO or provider-wide out-of-coverage history. All transformations must be deterministic, idempotent, tested for absence of lookahead and fully reversible to raw evidence.
