Use complete_acquisition_manifest.json and supplemental_source_quality_manifest.json as active allowlists; never glob roots containing preserved legacy files. Git intent distinguishes reviewable code/compact metadata from ignored private data, price cursors, examples and tests. Reused March raw remains an existing single copy.

Earlier inherited listing gate/scratch fields are historical states; final acquisition and cleanup receipts govern completion.

The inventory excludes itself and final_closeout_receipt.json to avoid circular hashes. The closeout receipt binds inventory, review card, session report and primary manifests.
