4. Compute safe canonical 20-stock future four-hour TG20 labels for train + development only
At each permitted origin, exactly 20 genuinely available, compatible, same-session future endpoints define price-only log returns
r[i,t] = log(C_genuine[i,exit] / C_genuine[i,entry])
then a 20-stock median and unscaled cross-sectional MAD at that same origin:
m[t] = median_i r[i,t]
a[t] = median_i abs(r[i,t] - m[t])
epsilon_train = max(1e-4, 0.1 * median_(permitted unique TRAIN origins) a[t]), or the exact approved new-source rule after verifying existing contract code and documenting any difference.
TG20[i,t] = (r[i,t] - m[t]) / (a[t] + epsilon_train).
