Exact entry points all in src/lstm_data/corporate_actions.py: reconcile_source:114 is metadata-only; normalize_action:219 requires explicit held role/ex-session boundary/sequence/share epoch/rounding; apply_actions:422 sorts declared boundary+sequence and rejects ties, detects mutated replay; settle_dividend:439 transfers signed accrual once; snapshot:506 returns signed financial statements and incomplete missing marks. SourceEvidence:43 preserves event/hash/retrieval/publication separately. Action:59, Position:280, DividendClaim:297, Account:329 and RawMark:477 are immutable typed records. Decimal math; no new dependencies or persistence ledger.

P3 caller supplies one net physical Position per symbol and explicit cash (including short-sale proceeds), stable position IDs, chronological as_of, action evidence and explicit pre/post raw share epochs. Opening/closing/fill/borrow/transaction costs are outside this package. Process ex-session actions against prior carried physical positions before new entries; already closed holdings are absent/zero. No position opened later can be retroactively qualified. Ex-boundary09:30ET and date-based simulated payment are declared modeling conventions, not actual record-holder/settlement chains. Caller verifies exchange session/date; constructors do not assert calendar or source truth.

Source records with unclear roles/conversion are rejected; acquirer cash-merger records do not pay held JNJ/WMT shares the acquiree rate. Rate epoch must match eligible shares, especially same-day split+dividend. The raw archive does not establish that epoch or fractional/cash-in-lieu rule; uncertain affected valuations remain ASSUMPTION_DEPENDENT/BLOCKED. Known retrieved payable date enables a date-based simulated transfer only, not proof of broker cash arrival. Unknown dates retain signed claims indefinitely; receivables survive later sales because entitlement is frozen. No taxes/withholding, lending recalls, broker settlement plumbing, interest, general cost/borrow or total-return target change implemented.

Raw marks require explicit supported observation and availability times, same raw share epoch and positive finite price. Stale permitted mark age is reported; not interpreted as current executable price. Caller remains responsible for evidence and stale-mark scope. Pending exit flag remains through actions; retry/mark/emergency/overnight liquidation policy awaits separate hourly-proxy amendment approval. Dividend-adjusted/total-return marks are rejected; no dividend cash added to TG0 price labels (labels not implemented).

Reproduce: README editable virtualenv setup, then python -m pytest tests/test_corporate_actions.py -q. Synthetic fixtures need no sources/secrets. Optional bounded 116-action test skips cleanly if ignored canonical archive is absent; it never downloads. For metadata reconciliation with retained data, read action JSON and manifest, verify manifest SHA, call reconcile_source(payload, sha256, datetime.fromisoformat(retrieved_at)); no prices/models/features consumed. No P3 simulator or revised P2.2A execution-provider activation from these primitives.
