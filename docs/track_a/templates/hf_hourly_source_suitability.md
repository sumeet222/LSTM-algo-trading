Adjustment HOLD: close_adj equals close in all observed rows. Re-backfill scheduling after actions is not an action-factor ledger or PIT-vintage certificate. Smooth split-boundary prices cannot establish O/H/L/C/volume parity or dividend adjustment. Do not mix close_adj with other OHLC fields to manufacture adjusted candles. Source-policy snapshots are pinned independently from the HF data revision; no publisher build-to-code attestation was supplied.
