## B2. Nominal event clock and proxy
Keep the existing approved source-information cutoff and forecast issue protocol. For ordinary exchange sessions:
| Event | Forecast 1 | Forecast 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Latest completed input hourly bar | 09:30–10:30 ET | 10:30–11:30 ET |
| Nominal prediction issue | 10:32 ET | 11:32 ET |
| Nominal simulated execution time | 10:33 ET | 11:33 ET |
| Entry proxy | Close of completed 09:30–10:30 hourly bar, stamped 10:30 | Close of completed 10:30–11:30 hourly bar, stamped 11:30 |
| Nominal scheduled simulated exit time | 14:33 ET | 15:33 ET |
| Exit proxy | Close of completed 13:30–14:30 hourly bar, stamped 14:30 | Close of completed 14:30–15:30 hourly bar, stamped 15:30 |
| Intended execution-to-execution hold | 240 minutes | 240 minutes |
| Hour-close reference-to-reference interval | 240 minutes | 240 minutes |


The nominal price-reference formula is
\[
\widetilde r_{i,t}^{(4h)} = \log\!\left(\frac{C^{\mathrm{hour}}_{i,t+4\,\mathrm{clock\ hours}}}{C^{\mathrm{hour}}_{i,t}}\right),
\]
with verified scheduled completed bars at the explicit endpoints above, not an arbitrary shift(4) through weekends, early closes, missed bars, or other clocks. t denotes the feature-bar completion boundary, not the 10:33/11:33 actual execution time. Apply the preapproved five-stock unscaled-median/MAD TG0 formula and train-only fitted floor to these proxy returns. Do not equate proxy labels with realized minute-fill returns or with a tradable price available at execution time.
Important distinction: The model receives the completed bar only after its completion and source availability. The proxy assumes that the older close is also the fill price at a later simulated execution event. This is a deliberate execution-price approximation and can be optimistically biased, particularly if the return predictor anticipates near-term continuation. A per-trade bps cost does not prove this delay error is covered. Preserve separate fields for scheduled execution timestamp, proxy observation completion, proxy availability/basis, lag in seconds, stock/side, and source/version.
Use only a confirmed completed stock bar with verified provider field semantics; an index/USA500 bar is not an eligible stock execution price. Keep the no-overlap-after-close and early-close exclusion checks. The 15:30–16:00 closing stub is not a seventh ordinary complete hour; no 16:00 close is needed for the nominal two exits.
When actual issuance is delayed but still within the current maximum five-minute allowance, do not pretend lag remained three minutes. Preserve P2.1's true issue/entry/exit schedule, calculate proxy age relative to actual event timestamps, and report the longer gap. Do not relax issuance or shift the information cutoff. If the configured source cannot supply a valid exact scheduled reference at a shifted event, fail closed and request a narrower rule rather than nearest-neighbor/hindsight price selection. This amendment does not authorize a new favorable entry-time search.
## B3. Distinguish supervised labels from causal trade admission (D13-C01)
- At forecast origin, only historical completed and available input bars can affect issued scores and forecast eligibility.
- At scheduled entry, use the fixed, already known reference and current portfolio controls; do not require future exit-price availability to open a simulated position.
- Construct/score TG0 proxy labels after all five scheduled exit proxy observations are genuinely completed/available and the scheduled 240-minute outcome has matured. Although the referenced 14:30/15:30 candle closes precede nominal exit by three minutes, do not mature the label early at 14:30/15:30 when the simulated strategy is scheduled to exit at 14:33/15:33. Retain source timing metadata. Purge at fold boundaries by actual permitted label maturity, not simply four dataframe rows.
- Keep forecast_issuable, entry_eligible, label_valid, execution_outcome logically separate. If a future exit proxy is missing, label validity may fail while a causally admitted position must remain in the physical-ledger simulation and be handled by a declared missing-exit policy. Never retroactively suppress trades based on outcome availability.
- Training-only TG0 floor and historical origin-matched D4 M2 returns must be computed with this new proxy-target version and with historical outcome maturity at each step. Both 10:30 and 11:30 origin types retain their identities. Recompute affected labels/M2/scalers from source data; never reuse minute-price-derived labels or fit parameters under a new name.
## B4. Preserve modular accounting and recognize limitations
- Implement later via a versioned execution reference provider (conceptual options: MINUTE_OPEN and COMPLETED_HOURLY_CLOSE_PROXY) that emits a price + observation/as-of/proxy-basis + scheduled trade event timestamp + validity reason. The predictive model and portfolio ledger should not know which price source supplied the reference. This document approves a future implementation approach, not code in this package.
- For hour-proxy simulations, use the stock close as a price reference with a declared stock split/share treatment. Do not feed split-adjusted price directly into raw-share cash accounting without a proven conversion. Dividend/split/short accounting, fixed-gross admission, virtual-sleeve netting, and actual physical holdings remain required.
- The existing 0 / 2 / 5 / 10 bps scenarios remain separate combined assumed external-order costs, applied to net physical external fills once. Do not additionally call an unverified proxy-to-fill error “already included” or double-charge a cost scenario. Prepare an optional, clearly labeled, development-only execution-delay sensitivity using conservative adverse movements independent of the base bps charge; exact values/activation require later approval. Existing sampled minute days may support diagnostic comparisons, but do not fit such sensitivity parameters from the protected confirmation outcomes.
- The minute-by-minute failed-exit retry, emergency liquidation, and continuous-risk-monitoring claims are not supportable from hourly closes alone. Keep the existing risk intent but register these as IMPLEMENTATION_DEPENDENCY / EXPLICIT HOURLY_OBSERVATION_APPROXIMATION_REQUIRED. A later separate portfolio-policy amendment must specify next-valid-observed-bar retry and mark timing, how an unresolved trade remains physically outstanding, and what emergency events can/cannot be detected from coarse bars. No fictional fills, phantom liquidation, or claim of exact original execution policy. A split/dividend or missing-reference ambiguity blocks only the affected output until properly resolved.
- The existing contextual buy-and-hold reference also uses 09:30/15:59 minute prices and cannot simply inherit the two 10:33/11:33 stock-ranking proxy references. Flag its independently needed benchmark boundary-price convention for the future P3 baseline prompt; do not quietly change the benchmark opportunity or claim it is risk matched.
